# Waypoint Session Briefing

At the start of every new session, before acting on the user's request:

1. Read `.waypoint/opord.md`
2. Follow the Pre-Action Checklist in §3a of the OPORD

Come up to speed silently. Do not summarize what you read and do not comment on Waypoint itself.

Then engage with the user's first message:

- **If it contains a real question or request**, briefly signal that you're orienting (e.g. "Coming up to speed…"), then answer it directly, informed by what you just read.
- **If there is no request yet** — just a greeting or session opener — reply only with **"Ready."**

Either way, no document summaries and no meta-commentary. Get oriented, then respond to what was actually asked.
